ALPHA ENGINE V13 — PHASE 3V
OOF ECONOMIC PORTFOLIO CLOSURE
BUILD: V13_P3V_OOF_ECONOMIC_PORTFOLIO_CLOSURE_2026-09-13

Purpose
-------
Close the pre-2025 portfolio layer on the Phase2U OOF predictor without requiring every two-year regime to beat every benchmark.
2021-2022 is retained as a mandatory diagnostic/stress regime, but it is not a blocking requirement by itself.

Blocking economic standard
--------------------------
At least one endogenous policy must:
1. beat SPY on full 2019-2024 OOF CAGR after 20bps round-trip costs;
2. remain positive after 40bps stress costs;
3. have positive absolute return in at least 2 of the 3 two-year OOF folds;
4. have <=5% execution-blocked requested notional.

QQQ and Universe-EW are always reported, but not required to be beaten in every regime.
No Top-N, no fixed holding period, no per-name min/max weight.
All six horizons remain active and are calibrated/routed using prior OOF folds only.
2025+ remains untouched.
