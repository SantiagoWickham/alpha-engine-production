# ALPHA ENGINE V13 — Return-First Multi-Horizon Mandate

V13 is a new challenger architecture. V12 remains archived as historical evidence and is never used as a predictor.

## Non-negotiable research contract

- Primary objective: maximize **pre-2025 net excess return**, not Sharpe or aesthetic diversification.
- Benchmarks: SPY, QQQ, and equal-weight research universe where available.
- Simultaneous horizons: 5, 10, 20, 60, 120, 252 sessions. No horizon is allowed to suppress the others merely because it wins a tournament.
- Forecast horizon is not a mandatory holding period. The advisor reevaluates every completed session.
- No fixed portfolio cardinality. Position count is endogenous to opportunity quality, net edge, costs, uncertainty, covariance and sparsity cost.
- No fixed 3% minimum or 12% maximum weight. Research may concentrate heavily when evidence supports it. Hard research cap is 100%; actual concentration must be earned by the optimization objective.
- No structural small-cap, large-cap, value, growth, sector or style preference. Style variables may survive only for incremental predictive value.
- V12 Phase4+ feature/model/policy/OOS outputs are denied as V13 research inputs.
- All V13 research selection must satisfy `signal_date < 2025-01-01`.

## 2025–2026 label

The V13 code will exclude 2025+ from research and can later evaluate it as a code-blinded holdout. However the researchers have already observed V12 outcomes from this interval, so it must not be described as a scientifically virgin holdout. The first truly virgin evaluation for V13 is forward data after the V13 freeze.
